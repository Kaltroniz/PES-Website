# Events & Activities

## 1. Vigyanotsav
**Where curiosity meets creativity.**

Vigyanotsav is Pehchaan Ek Safar’s science exhibition as part of science and environment outreach that gives school students a platform to build, experiment, and showcase their ideas. Students explore themes ranging from Information & Communication Technology, Life Sciences, Sustainable Energy, and Physical & Mathematical Sciences, to areas like Robotics & Mechanical Design, Smart Infrastructure & Environment, Electronics & Circuits, Computer Science & AI, Biomedical Innovations, and Agricultural Technology turning classroom concepts into working models and creative solutions.

The experience goes beyond the exhibition. Science stalls, interactive games, research exhibits, and scientific talks bring students face-to-face with the world of science and the researchers at IIT Ropar.

### What happens:
- Working science models
- Student demonstrations
- Interaction with IIT Ropar experts
- Science-based activities and experiments
- Exposure to different fields of science

More than a competition, Vigyanotsav is a celebration of curiosity, creativity, collaboration, and the joy of discovering how things work.

## 2. Sunshine Masti
Sunshine Masti is our annual 45-day summer camp – a part of Paathshala project where learning steps outside the classroom. Children spend their summer exploring art, culture, creativity, general knowledge, teamwork, and a whole lot of fun.

There are activities, games, crafts, conversations, and plenty of moments where children get to simply be children.

### What happens:
- Art and craft activities
- Games and team activities
- Cultural activities
- General knowledge sessions
- Creative challenges
- Collaborative learning

## 3. Invited Talk
**Meet people. Hear stories. Discover possibilities.**

Sometimes, one conversation can change the way a student looks at their future.

Through our Invited Talks, we bring faculty members, professionals, researchers, and mentors into conversations with students. These sessions go beyond textbooks to talk about careers, education, environment & climate, opportunities, experiences, and the realities of different paths.

The goal isn't to tell students what they should become. It is to help them discover what they could become.

### What happens:
- Career and academic guidance
- Interactions with faculty and professionals
- Exposure to different career paths
- Discussions on higher education and opportunities
- Open Q&A sessions

## 4. Festival Celebrations
**Different festivals. One community.**

Festivals are more than decorations and sweets. They are stories, traditions, colours, food, music, and opportunities to come together.

At Pehchaan, we celebrate both national and cultural festivals together creating rangolis, painting diyas, decorating spaces, sharing traditions, and learning about the cultures around us.

The idea is simple: we may celebrate differently, but there is always a reason to celebrate together.

### Some of our activities include:
- Independence Day & Republic Day Celebrations
- Diya Painting & Diya Stall with the message "One Diya One Donation" during Diwali to encourage creativity and raise funds
- Teacher’s Day celebration
- Festival-themed Art & Crafts
- Cultural Activities & Performances
- Stories & Conversations around Festivals such as Eid and Christmas

## 5. Zeitgeist Stall
At Zeitgeist, the annual cultural fest of IIT Ropar, Pehchaan Ek Safar proudly set up a stall that reflected both creativity and purpose. The stall showcased handmade items prepared by our Paathshala children and volunteers, serving as a window into the talent and potential that education unlocks.

### Our Motive
The primary aim of this initiative was to:
- Raise awareness about Pehchaan’s work and the importance of making education accessible to underprivileged children.
- Mobilize support and funds for ongoing projects that bring learning opportunities to those who need them most.

### Impact
The stall became a lively hub of interaction, where entertainment met empathy. Every conversation, every game, and every gift carried forward the message that education is a right, not a privilege. Through this effort, Pehchaan connected with the wider community, inspiring them to join hands in building brighter futures.

## 6. Let’s Talk (New Initiative)
**A space to pause. A space to listen. A space to be.**

Let's Talk is a small initiative where we get together over chai and talk about things that are simple enough for everyone to relate to, but deep enough to make us think. There are no speakers or experts here, and there are no right answers. We simply start with one question, share our experiences and perspectives, and see where the conversation takes us.

It is an open conversation space where students and scholars come together to talk about things that usually don't make it into a syllabus - beauty, books, relationships, memories, dreams, failures, perspectives, and life itself.

No lectures. No right answers. Just conversations, stories, and different ways of seeing the world.

### What happens:
- Open-ended conversations
- Personal stories and experiences
- Thought-provoking questions
- Book and idea sharing
- A space for listening without judgement
